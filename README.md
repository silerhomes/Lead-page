# Lead Page Starter

A single web page with a form on it. Someone fills it out, you get an email.

This is a starting point, not a finished page. It is deliberately plain so that making it yours is the whole exercise.

---

## The two things you must change

Everything else is optional. These two are not.

**1. Your Formspree ID.** In `index.html`, find `YOUR_FORM_ID` and replace it with your own. If Formspree gave you `https://formspree.io/f/abcd1234`, then your ID is `abcd1234`.

**2. Your contact info.** Your name, phone, and license number are in the footer near the bottom of the file. There is a placeholder phone number in there right now, and it is not yours.

---

## Making it look like you

Open the page in Claude and just describe what you want. You do not need to know CSS.

Things that work:

> Change the colors on this page to navy and warm cream, and use a serif font for the headings.

> Make this page feel warmer and less corporate. Softer colors, rounder corners.

> Rewrite the headline and the paragraph under it so it speaks to people going through a divorce.

> Change the dropdown options so they fit somebody who works mostly with first time buyers.

> Add a section under the headline with three short reasons someone should work with me.

> Make the whole thing feel like my Instagram: [describe your colors and vibe].

Near the top of `index.html` there is a block labeled **YOUR SETTINGS** with the colors and fonts in it. If you would rather change things by hand, that is the place. Everything on the page pulls from those values, so changing one line changes the whole page.

---

## Making it live

1. Settings, then Pages
2. Source: `main` branch, `/ (root)` folder
3. Save

Give it a minute or two, then your page is at:

```
https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/REPO-NAME
```

If you get a 404, it is almost always one of these: not enough time has passed yet, you did not save the Pages setting, or your file is not named `index.html`.

---

## Test it before you share it

Fill out your own form and submit it.

The first time anyone submits, Formspree emails you a confirmation link. **Until you click that link, submissions will not come through.** This trips up everybody once. Click it, submit one more time, and confirm the lead actually lands in your inbox.

Do not put the link in your bio until you have seen a test lead arrive.

---

## Where to put your link

- Instagram bio
- Email signature
- Texts to your sphere
- QR code on a sign or a pop-by card

---

## If you break it

You cannot permanently break anything. Every change is saved in GitHub's history, and you can always ask Claude to put it back the way it was.
